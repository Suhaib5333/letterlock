Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026 · Letterlock is made by RAL Technologies, Kingdom of Bahrain.

The short version

  • You can play Letterlock as a guest without giving us anything. Guest progress stays on your device.
  • If you create an account we store your email, username, XP and level, friends list, saved games and scores. Nothing else.
  • The mobile apps show ads (Google AdMob). You are asked for consent first where the law requires it, and you can choose non-personalised ads.
  • "Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • We never sell your data and we do not use it for anything other than running the game.
  • You can delete your account at any time, inside the app or at letterlock.raltech.dev/account/delete.
  • Letterlock is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children.

1. Who we are

Letterlock ("the game", "the app", "the website") is developed and operated by RAL Technologies, a company based in the Kingdom of Bahrain ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at legal@raltech.dev.

This policy covers the Letterlock website (letterlock.raltech.dev), the installable web app, and the Letterlock apps for iOS, Android and Android TV.

2. Playing as a guest

You do not need an account to play. When you play as a guest:

  • Your settings, in-progress match, and the "questions already seen" tracker are stored only on your device (browser storage or app storage). We do not receive them.
  • If you join an online room, the room code, the display name you type, and your answers are sent to our game server so the host's screen can show them. They are kept only for the life of the room (rooms are removed when the host ends the game or after a period of inactivity).
  • Guest play does not earn XP, friends or leaderboard entries, because there is no account to attach them to.

4. How we use your data

  • To run the game: sign you in, save your progress, show leaderboards, connect you with friends and rooms.
  • To keep the service safe: block abuse, enforce our Terms, rate-limit sign-in codes.
  • To honour your purchases: hide ads on every device where you are signed in.
  • To answer your support requests.

Our legal bases (where the GDPR or similar laws apply) are: performance of our contract with you (running the game you asked for), our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure, and your consent (for personalised advertising).

5. Advertising

The Letterlock mobile apps are free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads appear between games and on menu screens, never during a question. On the website, ads may later be shown through Google AdSense in the same places.

  • Consent. Before any ad is shown, the app displays a consent screen (Google's User Messaging Platform). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland this lets you accept or refuse personalised ads and see the list of ad partners. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency. On iPhone and iPad the app asks for permission before allowing ad tracking. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", you still see ads, but they are not personalised across apps.
  • What AdMob receives. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type and app usage signals to select and measure ads. This is governed by Google's privacy policy. We do not share your email, username or game data with ad networks.
  • No ads at all. Buy "Remove Ads" (section 6), or play on the website, where ads are not currently shown.

6. Purchases

"Remove Ads" is a one-time in-app purchase made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store processes your payment; we never receive your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to verify the purchase and to remember that your account has it. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt, an anonymous app-user identifier, and, when you are signed in, your Letterlock account identifier, so the entitlement follows your account to other devices and to the website. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from, under its rules (see our Terms).

7. Where your data is stored and who can see it

Account data is stored on our own backend, running on servers operated by RAL Technologies. Until our migration to that backend is complete, account data is processed by our hosted database provider, Supabase (supabase.com), under its data-processing terms. Access to the database is limited to the RAL Technologies team and is protected by per-user access rules, so one player can never read another player's private data.

8. Other players

Your username, level, rank badge and scores are visible to other players on leaderboards and in rooms you join. Your email address is never shown to anyone. Custom packs you publish carry your username as the author.

9. Children

Letterlock is intended for players aged 13 and over (or the higher minimum age in your country, for example 16 in some EU countries). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. The "Kids" question pack is content for families to play together on one screen; it does not change who the game is for. If you believe a child has created an account, email legal@raltech.dev and we will delete it.

10. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Online room data: until the room ends, at most a few hours.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Sign-in codes: expire within minutes and are then deleted.
  • Backups: encrypted backups are rotated within 30 days, so deleted data leaves backups within that time.

11. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time:

  1. In the app: Home → tap your username (👤) → Delete account → confirm.
  2. On the web: follow the steps at letterlock.raltech.dev/account/delete.
  3. By email: write to legal@raltech.dev from the email address on the account. We delete within 7 days and confirm by reply.

Deletion removes your profile, email, username, XP, friends, saved game, leaderboard entries and custom packs. Anonymous aggregate statistics (for example how many players answered a question correctly) contain no personal data and are kept. A "Remove Ads" purchase is tied to your store account and can be restored in a new Letterlock account through the store's "Restore purchases" button.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent (for personalised ads, from the app's Settings), and to complain to your local data protection authority. Bahrain's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 30 of 2018) gives residents of Bahrain equivalent rights. To exercise any right, email legal@raltech.dev. We answer within 30 days.

13. Security

All traffic between your device and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS and secure websockets). Passwords do not exist in Letterlock: sign-in uses one-time codes or Google / Apple. Sign-in codes are stored hashed and rate-limited. Database access is limited per user by server-side rules.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, show a notice in the app. Older versions are available on request.
