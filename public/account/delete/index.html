Delete your Letterlock account

Letterlock by RAL Technologies · Deletion is permanent and free.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: email address, username, XP, level, prestige and unlocked content.
  • Your friends list and pending friend requests.
  • Your saved (in-progress) game.
  • Your leaderboard scores and match results.
  • Custom question packs saved to your account.

Everything is removed immediately from our live database. Encrypted backups are rotated within 30 days. Anonymous aggregate statistics (for example how often a question was answered correctly) contain nothing about you and are kept.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Letterlock and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. On the Home screen, tap your username (👤) at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. You are signed out and the account is gone.

Open Letterlock

Option 2: by email

If you cannot sign in, or you would rather not open the app, email us from the address on the account:

Email legal@raltech.dev

Include your username if you remember it. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by reply. If the email comes from a different address we may ask you to prove the account is yours (for example by sending you a one-time code).

Before you go

  • Remove Ads purchase: it is tied to your App Store or Google Play account, not to your Letterlock account. After creating a new Letterlock account you can bring it back with Restore purchases in the app.
  • Guest data (settings, seen-questions tracker, a local saved game) lives only on your device. Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove it.
  • Deleting your account does not delete online rooms you are currently in; leave them first.
